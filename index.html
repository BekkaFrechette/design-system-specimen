Introduction

The global sensation manga and anime One Piece has been around for almost 30 years now, and in that time, with 10 different sagas, comprised of over 30 different arcs, there is only one that stands above the rest. As the story is reaching in on it’s final saga, everything has lead to some monumental reveals, and the time that the Straw Hat Crew (and the friends they made while there) have spent on the future island of Egghead lead to some of the most intense revelations seen in the story so far. World changing information that author Eiichiro Oda has spent most of his life building up to comes to the biggest head in this arc, unequivocally making it the most important arc yet.
